Investor brief · I-Corps track

Spring load restrictions, decided by data.

TERRA replaces calendar-based seasonal load posting with a continual, open-data ML engine that carries calibrated uncertainty on every number. This brief covers the wedge economics, verifiable traction and the path to first revenue.

The problem

Every cold-climate highway agency repeats the same expensive guess, every spring.

Freeze–thaw cycles weaken pavement foundations. Agencies must decide when to post seasonal load restrictions and when to lift them. Today that decision runs on fixed calendar dates, 1970s thaw-depth tables and phone calls. Post too early and haulers lose millions to detours. Lift too early and weakened pavements fail under legal loads. The problem repeats annually across roughly 25 US states and the Canadian provinces, and AASHTO's MEPDG formally requires seasonal resilient-modulus inputs, which is exactly the quantity TERRA predicts with calibrated uncertainty.

The solution

A continual engine, an audit trail, and engineers keep final sign-off.

TERRA Restriction ingests keyless public data: FHWA LTPP ground-truth tests, ERA5 reanalysis climate, USDA-NRCS SSURGO soil profiles, ISRIC SoilGrids texture and OpenStreetMap networks. A drift-aware ML core retraines itself continuously and issues per-section restriction advisories with 90% confidence bands and a versioned model registry that documents every promotion for the record.

Why now

LTPP reached maturity

Thirty-plus years of instrumented sections, including in-ground frost sensors (SMP_FROST_PENETRATION) and real section GPS, downloadable per state from a public CDN. Verified on SDR 40, September 2026.

MEPDG created formal demand

AASHTOWare adoption made seasonal Mr inputs a hard requirement in practice, not a nice-to-have.

The ML field is stuck on frozen models

Published geotechnical models freeze at publication. Our 52-week deployment simulation on real LTPP streams shows a 6.9% mean and 20% final-week RMSE advantage. Reproducible, in-repo.
