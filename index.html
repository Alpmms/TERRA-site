The wedge

We start with the one decision every highway agency makes every single spring.

Seasonal load posting is repetitive, high-stakes and stubbornly analog. District engineers read thaw-depth tables, phone neighboring districts and post restrictions by calendar date. Too early, and haulers pay millions in detours. Too late, and weakened pavements fail under legal loads. TERRA fixes this first, then expands into year-round geotechnical intelligence.

1
Advisory phaseTERRA Restriction ranks sections by thaw risk from live ERA5 climate and soil data. Engineers keep final sign-off. We make the guess informed.
2
Calibration phaseThe agency's own FWD deflection and frost-tube observations tune conformal intervals to local conditions.
3
Decision phaseRestriction windows issued per district with confidence bands, a full audit trail and automatic documentation for the record.
The platform

Three modules. One living geotechnical brain.

A single open-data heartbeat powers all three: ingest, harmonize, detect drift, retrain, promote. No frozen models, ever.

Wedge · Pilot

TERRA Restriction

Seasonal load-restriction advisories per section: freezing index, freeze–thaw cycles, soil frost susceptibility and thaw-stabilization outlook, all with confidence bands. The direct DOT budget saver, deployed first.

Year 1

TERRA Core

Resilient-modulus intelligence: predict Mr (MPa) from material properties, stress state and climate with 90% conformal intervals, plus a versioned registry that documents every promotion for compliance.

Year 2

TERRA Lens

Smartphone aggregate QC: a training-free Otsu and watershed pipeline turns site photos into gradation curves and angularity indices, robust to exposure, blur, JPEG and rotation. Quarry-to-paving matching on open maps.
