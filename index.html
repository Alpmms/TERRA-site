the wedge

We don't boil the ocean. We start with the one decision every highway agency makes every single spring.

Seasonal load posting is repetitive, high-stakes, and stubbornly analog: district engineers look at thaw-depth tables, call neighboring districts, and post restrictions by calendar. Too early costs haulers millions in detours. Too late destroys weakened pavements. This is the wedge — then TERRA Core and TERRA Lens expand into year-round geotechnical intelligence.

1
Advisory phaseTERRA Restriction ranks sections by thaw risk using live ERA5 + soil data. Engineers keep final sign-off — we make the guess informed.
2
Calibration phaseAgency's own FWD/deflection and frost-tube observations tune the conformal intervals to local conditions.
3
Decision phaseRestriction windows issued per district with confidence bands, audit trail, and automatic documentation for the record.

Built entirely on open data: FHWA Long-Term Pavement Performance program · ERA5 reanalysis via Open-Meteo · USDA-NRCS Soil Data Access (SSURGO) · ISRIC SoilGrids · OpenStreetMap contributors. Physics-calibrated synthetic records (Li & Selig 1994; Uzan/Witczak universal model) bootstrap the platform and are labeled as such; live ingestion progressively replaces them. Metrics are reported domain-wise and honestly. Scientific honesty is the product.
